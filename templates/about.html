College Engineering Project

Care Connect Platform Architecture

Designed as a modular, secure, full-stack healthcare coordination system connecting Hospital Management, Doctors, and Patients.

Core Problems Addressed

Specialist Discovery Bottlenecks

Patients often do not know which specialist matches their symptoms, causing diagnostic delays.

Fragmented Schedules & Queues

Manual doctor timetable maintenance leads to overbooking and long waiting hall times.

Lack of Digital Verification

Paper slips easily get lost. QR code verification gives instantaneous contactless confirmation.

Administrative Data Silos

Hospitals lack unified views of bed counts, doctor availability, and weekly appointment loads.

System Architecture

Presentation Tier
  • • HTML5 & Modern Semantic Layout

  • • Chart.js for real-time analytics

  • • ReportLab PDF generation ready
